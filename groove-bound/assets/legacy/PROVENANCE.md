## Classification

These are **legacy first-party project exports** used to restore visual identity
while the remake systems are rebuilt. They are isolated under `assets/legacy/`
so they can later be replaced or re-exported from Dropbox source files without
changing gameplay IDs.

The unrelated “Explosions and Powers Sprites” Dropbox pack was not imported
because its commercial-use provenance still needs verification.

The `m6x11plus.ttf` font retains its original embedded author metadata. Its
redistribution terms should be included in the final asset-license register
before public release.

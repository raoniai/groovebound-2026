# Groove Bound — First-Draft Canon

This is the genuine first canon pass for the playable two-stage campaign. It is
designed to be refined later without breaking stable stage, character, enemy,
or scene IDs.

## The world

Backbeat is a city where music is infrastructure. Train timing, street lights,
rooftop venues, and the defensive Pulse Tower all share a living network called
the Resonance. A small number of people can bind directly to it. They are known
as Resonants.

The Break begins when a cosmic chord plays backwards through every connected
speaker. Instrument-machine invaders descend into the city and turn familiar
sounds into hostile patterns. Their purpose is not simple destruction: they are
sampling Backbeat, mapping its Resonance, and assembling an orchestra for an
unknown intelligence.

## The heroes

### Joe — The Backbeat

Joe is a steady street guardian who knows how the city moves. He is durable,
powerful, and difficult to displace. His **Hold the Line** trait grants starting
Guard and stronger knockback. His signature starting weapon is the Kazoo
Pistol.

### Lyra Vex — The Live Wire

Lyra is a rock-and-roll explorer who treats danger like a stage entrance. She
is faster, fires more often, and earns additional Resonance XP, but has lower
vitality and defense. Her **Stage Dive** trait and Keytar Chord reward active,
risky movement.

Joe and Lyra experience the same campaign events while receiving a few
character-specific dialogue beats. Their strengths are intentionally
complementary rather than direct upgrades.

## Stage 1 — Backbeat Streets

The first invasion wave surrounds Pulse Tower. Concert equipment, service
alleys, and city infrastructure become the arena. Joe or Lyra must survive the
instrument-machine horde, defeat the Metronome Guardian, and break open the
Static Baron.

Inside the Baron is the **First Press**, a strange record that encodes a map as
music. Its signal points beneath the city to an abandoned orbital rail terminal.

## Stage 2 — The Orbit Line

The Orbit Line has been closed for years, but it is still broadcasting beyond
the atmosphere. Its platforms are fused with alien amplifiers, transit gates,
cosmic speakers, and impossible light.

The escalating enemy orchestra includes:

- Vinyl Drones, Trumpet Rays, Drum Wheels, and Theremin Jellies;
- elite Amp Hounds and Keyboard Centipedes;
- the Turntable Sentinel midboss;
- the megazord-like Grand Orchestrator final boss.

These enemies do more than chase: they charge, orbit, fire hostile notes, pulse
area attacks, and combine pressure patterns. The player's complete Stage 1
weapon and support build carries forward after a recovery beat.

## Ending hook

Defeating the Grand Orchestrator interrupts only the first movement. A distant
voice identifies itself as the Grand Conductor and begins assembling a larger
orchestra. Joe and Lyra decide that the next answer will not come from one hero,
but from the whole city.

## Canon boundaries

- The Resonance, the Break, Backbeat, Pulse Tower, First Press, Orbit Line,
  Resonants, Joe, Lyra Vex, and the current enemy roster are first-draft canon.
- Dialogue is intentionally replaceable and stored separately from campaign
  state.
- Current still-image cutscenes are production placeholders for later supplied
  videos.
- Existing audio remains unchanged and is not yet a canon soundtrack.

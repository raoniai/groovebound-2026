# Stage 2 Visual Prompt Set

All campaign artwork was generated with OpenAI image generation in Codex. No
external or third-party image was used as a reference. The existing Groove
Bound palette—deep indigo, cyan, teal, magenta, violet, gold, and warm red—was
the visual anchor across every prompt.

## Shared production constraints

Detailed illustrated 16-bit pixel art; crisp pixel clusters; strong dark
outlines; readable silhouettes at gameplay scale; consistent three-quarter or
top-down perspective; no text, letters, numbers, watermarks, UI, gradients in
chroma backgrounds, cross-cell overlap, or shadows crossing cells.

## Character action sheets

Strict 8-column by 4-row transparent-safe action atlas. Rows: down, up, left,
right. Columns: two idle frames, two walk frames, two faster run frames, two
hurt/action frames. Constant scale, feet alignment, lighting, identity, and
silhouette in every cell.

- **Joe:** warm brown skin, dark hair, teal bomber jacket, dark trousers, red
  shoes; grounded street-guardian energy.
- **Lyra Vex:** cosmic rock-and-roll adventurer, violet hair, cropped crimson
  jacket, cyan-black adventure gear, boots, compact keytar; fast live-wire
  energy.

## Character portraits

Strict 2-column by 1-row illustrated portrait atlas. Joe on the left and Lyra
on the right, each framed as a dynamic waist-up three-quarter hero portrait.
Urban supernatural skyline, cosmic stage lights, music-energy accents, equal
scale, matching lighting, generous safe padding, and strong facial continuity.

## Prologue cutscene atlas

Strict 2-by-2 cinematic pixel-art storyboard:

1. Backbeat rooftops and neon nightlife before the invasion.
2. The Break reversing through every speaker beneath a cosmic tear.
3. Pulse Tower emergency broadcast as instrument robots descend.
4. Joe answering the downbeat against the bright city.

## Campaign transition and ending atlas

Strict 2-by-2 cinematic pixel-art storyboard:

1. The First Press record recovered from the Static Baron.
2. Joe and Lyra reading its musical map.
3. The abandoned Orbit Line descending beneath Backbeat.
4. The Grand Orchestrator and distant Grand Conductor signal.

## Orbit Line enemies

Strict 4-column by 2-row isolated sprite atlas:

1. Vinyl Drone
2. Trumpet Ray
3. Drum Wheel
4. Theremin Jelly
5. elite Amp Hound
6. elite Keyboard Centipede
7. Turntable Sentinel midboss
8. Grand Orchestrator final boss

Normal enemies combine one readable instrument with one alien-robot body plan.
Bosses use layered speaker armour, turntable cores, articulated limbs, cosmic
energy, and megazord scale without borrowing an existing franchise design.

## Orbit Line environment

Strict 4-by-2 prop atlas. Top row contains collision-scale objects: alien
speaker pylons, sealed transit gate, turntable console, and cosmic drum reactor.
Bottom row contains decorative platform signage without text, suspended note
lights, cable constellations, and a holographic orbital route structure.

## Weapon projectile atlas

Strict 6-column by 4-row atlas containing 24 different projectile silhouettes.
Each cell represents a different musical weapon or evolution: brass notes,
speaker shockwaves, cymbal blades, feedback loops, drum pulses, trumpet cones,
vinyl sparks, synth waves, triangle traces, cello lances, orbiting maracas,
tuning-fork bolts, keytar chords, bell rings, cassette echoes, laser-harp
beams, and the eight brighter evolved forms.

## Combat effects atlas

Strict 4-by-4 atlas containing a readable animation vocabulary: four impact
sparks, four explosions, four death/flicker bursts, and four enemy/player damage
responses. Effects use cyan, gold, magenta, violet, and warm white with no
ground plane or baked background.

World Tour

After the Prologue, take one build through genre-based worlds. Each world has two stages, its own enemies and bosses, a gameplay mechanic, a grade, and permanent rewards.

Included in v0.7.0 Funk, Soul, and Disco are playable in the public Mac download. Six further routes remain in development.

Each world adds a gameplay mechanic.

The current systems cover world selection, timing mechanics, grades, rewards, permanent perks, and three save slots.

Open chests for rewards.

Musical chests roll five hidden reels and give one, three, or five rewards. World bosses drop an Encore Gate chest that completes the stage and moves the run forward.

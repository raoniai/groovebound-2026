Backbeat was built on a living musical network.

The Resonance synchronises transport, street lights, rooftop venues, speakers, and Pulse Tower. A few people can bind to it directly. They are called Resonants.

Then a cosmic signal plays through the system in reverse. The event becomes known as the Break. Familiar sound is sampled, weaponised, and returned to the city through instrument-machine invaders.

The fiction lives inside every system.

Resonance gems carry XP. Weapons turn musical gestures into patterns. Supports change how a Resonant moves through pressure. Evolutions fuse both parts into a new instrument.
